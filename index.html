Week 7 · Jul 7 (2 Days After Retest)
🫀 Lactate Threshold Test
Finds your TRUE HR zones based on your actual physiology.
More accurate than any age-based formula.
Protocol
— Rest well the night before. No hard training day before.
— Light snack 45 min before. Hydrate well.
— Warm up 10 min easy walk/jog. Don't track this.
— Start a FRESH workout on Apple Watch.
— Run ALL OUT for exactly 20 minutes. Same effort as Cooper test.
— Start controlled → build → push hard final 5 minutes.
— Turn off HR alerts — ignore them completely during test.
— End workout at exactly 20 minutes. Check average HR = your LT2.
— 5 min cool down walk after.
Calculate Your True Zones
Zone 1 → LT2 × 0.77 and below
Zone 2 → LT2 × 0.77 to 0.83 ← New training zone
Zone 3 → LT2 × 0.83 to 0.94
Zone 4 → LT2 × 0.94 to 1.0
Zone 5 → above LT2
Example: LT2 avg HR = 172 → Zone 2 ceiling = 172 × 0.83 = 143 BPM
Update your HR alert after this test. Train with true zones from Week 7 onwards. 💙
